Graduate study

The lab’s research spans psychology and linguistics, with questions about discourse reference, space, attention, neuroscience, pragmatics and human perceptions of AI.

If these questions connect with your interests, email Amit Almor with a short introduction, your research interests and relevant experience. Please ask about availability for the application cycle you are considering.

Undergraduate research

Undergraduate students interested in language and cognition are welcome to inquire about research opportunities. Depending on the project, research can involve preparing materials, running studies, working with data and discussing scientific questions.

In your message, tell us what interests you, any relevant coursework or experience, and your availability. Specific activities and opportunities depend on current projects.

Start with a research question

Explore the research pages to identify an area that interests you. You do not need to know your eventual research topic before making an initial inquiry.
